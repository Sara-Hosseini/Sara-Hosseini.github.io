Featured Projects

Product Analytics: Funnel, Retention and A/B Testing

SQLPythonPower BIA/B testingCohorts

End-to-end case study on a simulated European mobility app, built on a synthetic dataset I generated and validated

Business question: Where do users drop out before booking, which acquisition channels bring valuable users, and should a simplified booking flow be rolled out?

What I did: Generated and validated 5,000 users and 141,813 events (23 validation checks, 25 pytest tests). Wrote 10 SQL analysis scripts (1,746 lines with the schema) covering KPIs, funnel, D1/D7/D30 retention, cohorts, segmentation and the experiment. Analysed the data in six Python notebooks and built a six-page Power BI report with 27 DAX measures.

Key findings:

  • The biggest leak is search to booking: 52% of users who search never start a booking, while 86.5% of started bookings complete. That points to fixing search and comparison, not checkout.
  • Referral users converted at 40.0% versus 23.5% for Paid Search, so channel quality matters more than volume.
  • The simplified booking flow raised conversion from 26.89% to 30.45%: +3.55 percentage points (95% CI 1.05 to 6.06 pp, p = 0.006). ARPU rose from €7.02 to €8.80.

Probabilistic Stock Price Forecasting (M.Sc. Thesis)

PythonForecastingPrediction intervalsNLP

"A Hybrid Deep Learning Framework for Stock Price Prediction Integrating Quantile Regression and Sentiment Analysis", IU International University of Applied Sciences, grade 1.8

Question: Can we forecast a realistic range for future prices (10th, 50th and 90th percentile) instead of a single number, and does news sentiment help?

What I did: Collected and cleaned 11 years of daily prices for AAPL, MSFT and NVDA plus about 1,200 to 1,350 news articles per stock. Built a Quantile LSTM with sentiment from a LoRA-fine-tuned FinBERT model, and evaluated it with five-fold walk-forward validation against XGBoost and naive baselines.

  • The 1-day prediction intervals hit 78.8% to 83.0% coverage against an 80% target, versus 70.1% for the XGBoost baseline.
  • Fine-tuning lifted sentiment macro F1 from 0.31 to 0.80.
  • Sentiment raised 10-day directional accuracy on news-event days to 72.2% (58.2% overall), while price error stayed close to a random walk, which is typical for large-cap stocks. The model's main value is calibrated uncertainty.
